# Interview Guide

## Core story

The design problem was not simply to automate three workflows. It was to preserve one existing commitment-control solution, add two independently governed customer-outcome modules, and reuse only controls that could be shared without mixing business rules or measurement denominators.

## Questions to prepare

- Why are technical restoration and customer-confidence recovery separate?
- Why is Customer Momentum not described as churn prediction?
- What decisions remain deterministic, what can AI contribute, and what requires human approval?
- What defect was found before Service Recovery import and how was it corrected?
- Why does the current evidence support Portfolio Preview rather than a stronger tier?
- What tests would be run before attaching live Salesforce or model-provider credentials?
