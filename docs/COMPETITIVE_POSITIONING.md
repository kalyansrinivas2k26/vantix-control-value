## Safe differentiation statement

The demonstrated portfolio differentiation is the explicit composition of evidence records, deterministic action policy, bounded AI explanation/critique, human consequential authority, separate technical/customer verification and fail-closed closure routing.

This does not establish exclusivity, commercial superiority or exhaustive market coverage.
