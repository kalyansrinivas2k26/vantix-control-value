# Agile Traceability

Control Value does not replace the Product Owner, Scrum Master, delivery team or Customer Success Manager.

It can expose whether promised outcome criteria are explicit, evidence exists, implementation activity is complete, customer outcome is separately verified, and unresolved outcome risk should remain open or escalate.

It does not claim AI sprint commitment, Product Owner authority, authoritative story points, velocity improvement, or that task completion equals customer value.

The portfolio owner's **Certified ScrumMaster** credential is not the same role as workflow `CSM_` action codes, which refer to Customer Success Manager.
