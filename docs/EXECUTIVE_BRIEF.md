# Executive Brief

**Business outcome:** Prevent customer commitments from being closed without evidence that the promised outcome was achieved and authorised for closure.

## Situation

CRM and delivery systems can show activity completion without proving customer-outcome completion.

## Complication

Customer-success platforms already support success plans, outcome tracking, health/risk signals and customer-success guidance. Public Gainsight materials describe success plans tied to business outcomes, while Salesforce Success Plans provide customer-success guidance and proactive monitoring. The reviewed materials do not establish that those products lack equivalent controls, so this project does not claim a market-exclusive capability.

## Question

How can a customer commitment move from promise to closure without allowing activity completion, unsupported AI interpretation, or incomplete evidence to become a false customer-outcome claim?

## Answer

Use an evidence-governed control chain: deterministic intake and assessment; evidence observation; bounded diagnosis and critique; deterministic output validation and action policy; human approval for consequential action; separate technical and customer-use verification; and fail-closed closure/escalation logic.

## Evidence

- credential-free 20-node synthetic governed outcome workflow;
- separate error workflow;
- later stored Foundation and Gate 2 validation reports recorded as 103/103 each;
- historical 5 August Control Value n8n execution evidence identified in retained evidence inventories;
- prior Project 3 records state three defects were found and fixed with permanent regression tests.

## What This Doesn't Prove Yet

No production-scale throughput, live Salesforce, live Gemini, authenticated production human approval, real customer outcome, durable production idempotency/DLQ, statistically stable process capability, external certification, or production readiness is proved.

## Roadmap

Close evidence-backed gaps only: final live GitHub CI after restoration, stronger runtime/adversarial evidence, authenticated approval integration if later implemented, demo recording, and genuine business/adoption evidence if the project is used beyond portfolio validation.
