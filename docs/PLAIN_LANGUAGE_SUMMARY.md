# Plain-Language Summary

A customer commitment can look complete in CRM even when the promised outcome was never proven. A task may close, a deployment may succeed, or a customer may be contacted without establishing that the original promise was fulfilled.

VANTIX Control Value separates **activity** from **verified customer outcome**. Deterministic rules control facts, permitted actions, verification and closure eligibility. AI can explain and critique at the synthetic Gate 2 boundary, but it cannot silently approve action or closure. Closure requires technical evidence, customer-use evidence, SLA/outcome evaluation and a valid human-approval contract.

The project is a Portfolio Preview. It does not claim live Salesforce, live Gemini, real customer outcomes or production readiness.
